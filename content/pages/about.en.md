This site is a living notebook. I am a computational neuroscientist studying
how populations of neurons represent uncertainty, and I use this place to
publish the parts of research that papers leave out: half-finished intuitions,
negative results, tooling notes.

## How this site works

Everything here is drafted in **Notion**, exported to markdown by
[elog](https://elog.1874.cool), validated against a typed content contract,
and rendered as a fully static site. The stack is open source — the same
template can publish your site too.

## Contact

The fastest way to reach me is email. I read everything, and I especially
enjoy hearing about experiments that *failed* in interesting ways.
